How it stays motivating for everyone

Style = who you are, self-chosen. The six PE Styles are your own choice about how you like to play — none is "better." It sets your card's colour and identity.
Tier = how you've grown, earnable by all. Bronze → Silver → Gold comes from the best of: growth, filling out your hexagon (breadth), mastery/leadership, or holding a high level (consistency). So a maxed-out kid earns Gold by breadth, consistency or mentoring — never a dead-end.
Effort is a full axis — the one every kid controls, on the hexagon next to the physical stuff.
No overall score. It's a shape, not a rating. Different shapes, not a ranking.
Photo = Google sign-in avatar. The student's existing FIS profile photo, shown automatically — never uploaded or stored by the card, never leaves FIS. Initials show if there's no photo. (Requires the card to live in an FIS-login app.)
